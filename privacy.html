Squish & Shape: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Squish & Shape does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your progress: levels finished, stars, coins, hearts, boosters and how your shop looks.
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and colour-blind symbols.
  • A play log: a short record of how levels went (for example, which level was played, how many moves were used, and whether it was won). It helps the developer understand where the game is too hard or too easy. You can switch it off in Settings under "Play log".

All of this is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing the play log

The play log leaves your device only if you choose to share it: tapping "Copy" in Settings puts it on your clipboard, and it goes wherever you paste it. It contains no name, email, device identifier or location.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services. The game does not connect to the internet.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Purchases

If a future version offers in-app purchases, payment is handled by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details. This policy will be updated before any such change.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Testing with TestFlight

If you test the game through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share information with the developer, such as the email you were invited with, crash reports and feedback you send through TestFlight. That is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
